Moonlight Sorrow

A gothic action platformer for the NES, running in NES-Emulator compiled to WebAssembly.

Sound starts when you press play. Keyboard or gamepad.

Controls

NES buttonIn the gameKeyboardGamepad
D-padWalk, crouch← ↑ ↓ →D-pad or left stick
AJumpARight or top face button
BAttackSBottom or left face button
StartStart the gameEnterStart
SelectStart the gameSpaceBack / Select

Player 2 uses I J K L, G (A), F (B), right Shift (Start) and right Ctrl (Select), the same keys as the desktop emulator. Ctrl+R resets the console.
